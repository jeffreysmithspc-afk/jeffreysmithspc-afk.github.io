RELEASE OF LIABILITY AND ASSUMPTION OF RISK, MEDICAL RELEASE, AND PHOTOGRAPHIC RELEASE

  1. 1.
    Mr./Ms.                   , [full legal name as it appears on passport] hereinafter referred to as the "Volunteer," acknowledges that traveling to Honduras and participating in social service projects with Impact Water Inc., its officers, directors, managers, employees, and legal representatives, including without limitation Mark F. Smith, Jose Yanez, and Jeffrey Alan Smith (collectively, "Impact Water Inc"), is undertaken voluntarily and at Volunteer's own risk, and that Impact Water Inc bears no responsibility or liability whatsoever in connection therewith, except as required by law.
  2. 2.
    Assumption of the Risks. Volunteer is aware and acknowledges that performing volunteer social service projects with Impact Water Inc may subject Volunteer to a number of risks, including the risks of injury, illness, or death. Volunteer understands and acknowledges that the risks include, without limitation: risks involved in transportation to, from, and within Honduras; risks that adequate medical facilities may not be available should Volunteer require medical attention; and risks of residing in and being subject to the laws of a foreign country. Volunteer is aware of the conditions that presently exist in Honduras, specifically, but not limited to, social unrest, security concerns, unsanitary health conditions in camps, villages, and municipalities, and the prevalence of hepatitis, malaria, cholera, dengue, and other diseases. Volunteer hereby assumes all risks associated with participating in social service projects in Honduras.
  3. 3.
    General Release. Volunteer hereby releases and forever discharges Impact Water Inc from any and all claims and demands of whatever kind or nature, whether known or unknown, arising out of or connected in any way with Volunteer's travel to and stay in Honduras and Volunteer's voluntary participation in the work of Impact Water Inc or any other organization. Impact Water Inc shall not be liable for any personal injury, loss of life, property damage, accident, delay, inconvenience, change in itinerary or accommodations, or other irregularity that may occur for any reason, including due to: (1) wrongful, negligent, or arbitrary acts or omissions by any third party or authority; or (2) without limitation, Acts of God, fire, acts of governments or other authorities, wars, civil disturbances, riots, strikes, thefts, pilferage, epidemics, quarantines, or dangers incident to sea, land, and air travel. Volunteer further understands that, by signing this Release, Volunteer is expressly releasing Impact Water Inc FROM ANY NEGLIGENCE OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE committed by Impact Water Inc that results in bodily injury or monetary loss to Volunteer.
  4. 4.
    Release Relating to Medical Treatment. Volunteer hereby releases and forever discharges Impact Water Inc from any and all claims and demands of whatever kind or nature, whether known or unknown, arising out of or connected in any way to first aid, medical treatment, or medical services rendered to Volunteer in the course of the trip to Honduras or otherwise related to the activities of Impact Water Inc.
  5. 5.
    Volunteer's Responsibility. It is Volunteer's responsibility to obey all local and international laws. Impact Water Inc does not condone any type of illegal activity. Volunteer has sole responsibility for providing required documentation to federal and local authorities, which may include, without limitation, passport, travel itinerary, photographs, and similar documents. The decision to obtain any vaccinations, medications, or other preventive measures prior to traveling to Honduras is the sole responsibility of Volunteer.
  6. 6.
    No Insurance or Other Employment Benefit. Volunteer understands and acknowledges that Volunteer is not an employee of Impact Water Inc. Volunteer understands and agrees that Impact Water Inc is under no obligation to provide, and will not provide, workers' compensation, malpractice insurance, or any employee benefit of any kind. Impact Water Inc will not provide travel insurance, accident insurance, death insurance, or insurance of any kind. Obtaining insurance coverage is the sole responsibility of Volunteer.
  7. 7.
    Binding Effect. Volunteer understands and acknowledges that this is a legal and binding document that affects Volunteer's legal rights and remedies. Volunteer agrees that the obligations herein shall be binding upon Volunteer personally, as well as Volunteer's heirs, executors, administrators, and family members.
  8. 8.
    Governing Law/Forum. This Release shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas. In the event of any claim or legal action against Impact Water Inc, Volunteer agrees that the claim or action will be filed in the State of Texas and hereby waives any right to commence litigation, administrative action, or arbitration relating to Volunteer's travel to and stay in Honduras, or participation in the work of Impact Water Inc, in any jurisdiction other than the State of Texas.
  9. 9.
    I, the Volunteer, certify that I have read all of the above and fully understand its content. I agree to hold Impact Water Inc harmless for any accidents, claims, losses, damages, or liabilities, including death, disability, or injury, that might occur. I expressly assume all risks with respect to the activities and circumstances described herein, and I pledge not to sue Impact Water Inc on account of any losses, claims, costs, liabilities, or damages. I agree not to claim the unenforceability of this Release.
  10. 10.
    Medical Release Information. I hereby authorize Impact Water Inc and/or its representatives to initiate any medical attention necessary on my behalf, including related and appropriate air or ground transportation in the event I am incapable of presenting myself for such care. I agree to be financially responsible for any care provided and for the requisite transportation, and I authorize the release of any medical and/or insurance-related information pertinent to the circumstances.
  11. 11.
    Photographic Release. I knowingly and willingly consent to the unrestricted, free use in any form of any photographs, interviews, films, videotapes, or other visual or auditory recordings, in any medium, including the internet, of me that Impact Water Inc or others may create in connection with my participation in this mission trip. I waive any right I may have or come to have to inspect or approve the finished project. I acknowledge and agree that I am not entitled to any compensation for the creation or use of the finished project.
  12. 12.
    Severability. If any provision of this Release is held to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
